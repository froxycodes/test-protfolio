About the practice

Focused on gum health and long-term oral care.

Dr. Himangi Dubey is a periodontist based in Lucknow, providing specialized care for gum health and dental implants at the Ajanta Hospital ABC Complex.

Periodontists receive advanced training in the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the gums and supporting structures of teeth.

✓Personalized periodontal treatment planning
✓Implant-focused gum and bone assessment
✓Minimally invasive and laser-assisted approaches where appropriate
Specialized services

Care designed around your gum and implant needs.

A focused range of periodontal and implant-related treatments, tailored to individual needs.

01 / GUM HEALTH

Gum Disease Treatment

Care for gum conditions ranging from gingivitis to periodontitis, including scaling, root planing and laser-assisted periodontal therapy where appropriate.

02 / IMPLANTS

Dental Implants

Implant treatment planning and placement focused on healthy supporting gum and bone tissue for long-term stability.

03 / AESTHETICS

Cosmetic Gum Corrections

Procedures such as crown lengthening, gum grafting for recession and aesthetic gum corrections to support a balanced smile.

04 / REGENERATION

Bone Regeneration

Treatment approaches intended to rebuild lost bone structure where clinically appropriate, including in implant-related cases.
